# Third-party data notices

## Apple TAC device mapping

The free local Apple TAC mapping used by Lager iPhone is derived from the public **MoazEb/tac-database** project:

- Source: https://github.com/MoazEb/tac-database
- License: MIT
- The Lager copy is filtered to Apple TAC entries and normalized for model display.

Blacklist verification is not inferred from TAC data. Lager links users to Swappa's separate free IMEI checker for manual verification.
